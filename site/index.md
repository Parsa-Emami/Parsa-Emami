## Principles

1. **Read — Find the real system.** Before changing code, trace the rule, the data, the state, and the place where reality actually lives.
2. **Model — Make complexity visible.** Turn hidden constraints into explicit boundaries, contracts, domain rules, and behavior people can reason about.
3. **Break — Think like the failure.** Attack paths, misuse cases, edge conditions, and operational pressure expose what a happy-path diagram never will.
4. **Prove — Ship with evidence.** Tests, observability, deterministic outputs, and release discipline turn confidence from a feeling into a property.
